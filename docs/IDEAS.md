# Ideas

These are sparks, not assignments. Pick one, change it, or ignore all of them.

| Theme | Idea 1 | Idea 2 | Idea 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Build for Your Campus** | Photo of a notice board in, calendar events out. | Explain a confusing lab question in simple words, with a hint but not the answer. | A mess-menu or timetable helper: "what is free on Thursday afternoon?" |
| **Build for Small Communities** | Turn a photo of a handwritten price list into a clean shared sheet for a local shop. | Plain-language helper for a government form, with the steps listed. | A tool that summarises a long community WhatsApp thread into decisions and to-dos. |
| **Build for Everyday Life** | Photo of a receipt in, split-the-bill answer out. | "What can I cook with this?" from a photo of your fridge. | Read a medicine or product label and explain it simply (with a "ask a doctor" reminder). |
| **Build for Learning** | Photo of a maths problem in, step-by-step hints out (not the full answer). | Make a short quiz from a page of your notes. | Explain a concept three ways: simple, with an analogy, and with an example. |
| **Build for Play and Creativity** | A story game where the model is the narrator and tools roll the dice. | Photo in, funny caption or a short poem out. | A "which character are you" quiz that reads a photo of your desk. |
| **Build for Builders** | A new tool for the harness (for example a safe web-page summariser you write yourself). | Add memory and a step log to the harness. | A small helper that reviews a code file and suggests tests. |

Tip: a small idea that works live beats a big idea that does not. Aim for one clear screen.
